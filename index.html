The Mark Server

A personal-use utility for maintaining an organized local reference copy of the owner's Google Drive documents for the creative writing and worldbuilding project The Mark.

How it works

  • The owner signs in through Google OAuth to authorize read-only access to Google Drive files and metadata.
  • An owner-operated Linux server uses rclone to download copies of files from a specifically designated project folder into local storage.
  • The original Drive files remain under the owner's control. This integration does not request permission to edit, upload, share, or delete them.
  • Local organization, indexing, document analysis, and version tracking are handled separately from the public website.

Who uses this application?

This is an owner-operated tool, not a public service. It has no public registration, no third-party users, and no mechanism for visitors to upload or access creative-project documents.

Google Drive permissions

The OAuth permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. Google grants this scope across the authorized Drive; the application's intended operations are restricted to the owner's designated project folder. The scope permits reading and downloading but not modifying or deleting Drive files.

Privacy and support

For details about what information is accessed, stored, and retained, see the privacy policy. For questions, contact the application operator through the support email shown in Google's authorization screen.
